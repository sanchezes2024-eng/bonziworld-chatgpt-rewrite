What the heck is this?

BonziWORLD Chatgpt rewrite is a BonziWORLD server written entirely from scratch with chatgpt! Its not finished yet, so everyone appears as a colored square as a placeholder for BonziBUDDY.

Getting Started

  1. Enter your name.
  2. Enter a room ID. The default room is default.
  3. Press Submit.

Moving

Once you're inside a room, your character appears as a purple square.

Drag your square around the desktop to move. Your position is synchronized with everyone else in the same room.

Chatting

Type a message into the taskbar at the bottom of the screen.

Press the Start button to send it. You can also press Enter.

Your message appears above your character as a speech bubble.

Text-to-Speech

Open Settings in the top-left corner to choose between:

  • Browser TTS
  • eSpeak

Messages are read aloud using the selected speech system.

Commands

/color [color] - Change your color!

  • If you use color without specifying a color, you will be given one at random.

Rooms

Players can join different rooms by entering different room IDs on the login screen.

Players only see and interact with people who are in the same room.
